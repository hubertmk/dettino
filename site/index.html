1How it works

From your voice to your cursor.

One trip, five stops. Nothing to open, no window to write in: the text arrives in the app you were already using.

  1. Hold

    Ctrl + Space, in any app

    Mail, chat, documents, the code editor, a form in the browser. The microphone keeps the last 1.5 seconds, so your first word is never clipped.

  2. Talk

    Say it the way you’d say it

    Ums, restarts and spoken numbers are fine. Say “comma” or “new paragraph” if you like; the obvious punctuation comes by itself. The recording is kept safe until the text lands.

  3. Let go

    Transcribed

    On your computer with Parakeet or Whisper, or by the speech provider you picked. 7.4 seconds of speech took about 230 ms on the development PC’s processor.

  4. Cleanup

    Cleaned, not rewritten

    Fillers go; numbers, names and punctuation get fixed; your word list spells what only you say. If the cleanup would change too much, Dettino pastes exactly what you said instead. Local cleanup on a laptop graphics card: about 240 ms.

  5. Pasted

    Right where you were typing

    The text lands at your cursor and your clipboard is put back the way it was. Changed your mind? One shortcut undoes the cleanup, another pastes the last one again.

Times measured warm on the development machine. Yours depend on your hardware and the providers you choose.

2Models

You choose who hears you.

Every dictation makes two stops: one model turns sound into words, another tidies them. Keep both on your computer, send them to providers with your own keys, or mix.

Audio and text never leave this computer. Setup downloads the speech model and installs a local cleanup model for you, with one progress bar. On Windows, best with a dedicated graphics card: about 1 to 2 seconds after you let go.

The recording stays here unless on-device transcription fails; only the text goes to your cleanup provider. About a second, and you pay for cleanup tokens only.

Nothing to install: one OpenAI key covers both stops. Audio goes to your transcription provider, text to your cleanup provider. The speed depends on your connection; under a second on a good one.

Or pair any providers yourself in Settings, with automatic fallback when one fails. Keys stay in the macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager.

Nothing leaves your computer unless you send it.

  • No account, no telemetry, no analytics, no backend. Dettino never takes screenshots.
  • Only what you send to providers you set up leaves the machine. “What would be sent?” shows the exact payload for any app.
  • Never read: password fields, browser address bars, and apps you mark “Never dictate”.
  • Screen context is yours to limit. Send it only to providers on this computer, or switch it off.
  • History is encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305. The key and your API keys live in the system keychain.
  • The clipboard is borrowed for a moment, kept out of clipboard history, and put back.

3Shortcuts

Five keys to know.

You’ll use the first one all day. Change any of them in Settings. On a Mac, Ctrl is Control and Alt is Option.

  • CtrlSpace

    Hold to talk

    Let go and it’s pasted. A quick tap still does what it always did in your apps.

  • CtrlShiftSpace

    Hands-free

    Talk as long as you like, up to 20 minutes. Space finishes, Esc throws it away.

  • CtrlAltSpace

    Edit by voice

    Select some text and say what should change.

  • CtrlAltZ

    Undo the cleanup

    Get exactly what you said instead, up to a minute later.

  • CtrlAltV

    Paste the last one again

    For when the cursor moved before the text arrived.

And the small things.

  • Your words

    Names and terms only you use, spelled right. Dettino suggests the ones it saw you correct.

  • Snippets

    Say a short phrase, get your address, a signature or the reply you send every week.

  • App rules

    Settings per app, including never dictating in one at all.

  • History

    Every dictation, raw and cleaned, encrypted on your computer. Retry from there if a provider was offline.

  • The line

    A thin line at the bottom of the screen moves with your voice. Hover it to pause, undo or paste the last one. Drag it anywhere.

  • Pasted as heard

    If the cleanup would change too much, you get your own words, untouched. Nothing is lost.

5FAQ

Questions.

Anything else: ciao@dettino.com

Does it work offline?

In Private mode, yes: both models run on your computer. Setup needs a connection once, to download them.

Which apps does it work in?

Any app where you can type: the browser, Word, Outlook, Slack, a code editor, Notepad. Dettino pastes at the cursor. One exception on Windows: if an app runs as administrator and Dettino doesn’t, Windows blocks the paste, so the text waits on your clipboard instead.

Do I need a graphics card?

Not required, but on Windows it makes a real difference. Speech recognition runs on the processor either way. The local cleanup in Private mode is where a dedicated graphics card helps: about a quarter of a second on a laptop graphics card, noticeably slower without one. On a PC without one, setup tells you, and Balanced or Cloud is the better fit. On a Mac, Apple Silicon runs the local models itself.

Which languages can I dictate in?

That depends on the speech model. Parakeet, the fast on-device default, is for English. Whisper, on your computer or in the cloud, understands many more.

Which Macs are supported?

Macs with Apple Silicon. The setup asks for three permissions with live status: Microphone, Accessibility (for the shortcut, pasting and screen context) and, on some macOS versions, Input Monitoring.

What does the keyboard hook see?

Dettino watches for your shortcut only. Every other key passes straight through and is never stored, logged or sent anywhere.

Why “Dettino”?

From the Italian dettare, to dictate. When setup is done, the line at the bottom of your screen says Pronto!
